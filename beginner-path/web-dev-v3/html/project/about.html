My Blog

I dont have a good name for my blog yet. But ever since I found out about the idea of blogging(this happened when I first found out about the Study Hacks blog by Cal Newport) I also wanted one. A corner on the internet that you can design for yourelf and you own the posts and whatever the decisions you make there, unlike an instagram profile where your data is being sold and you have don't have the choices you can make on a blog that you own. A blog could be like a diary of your thoughts but it happens to be on the internet that you are okay to share. I just like the aeshetics of a blog the posts, the cool looking images and you are the creator of all that! That's a special feeling.
